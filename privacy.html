Privacy Policy

Deploy Automation · Last updated November 23, 2026

Deploy Automation is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own use. This policy explains what data it handles.

Data we collect

Only the information entered into the owner's form: a company name, industry, goal, and challenge. No accounts, cookies, or analytics are used.

How data is used

  • Form inputs are sent to an AI language model provider and a web search provider to research and write a report.
  • The finished report is uploaded as a .docx file to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Processing logs are kept by the job-hosting service (Trigger.dev) for troubleshooting.

Google user data

Google Drive access is used solely to create report files in the owner's chosen folder. The app does not read, share, sell, or transfer Google user data, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Reports stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
